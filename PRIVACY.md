# Valrove privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Valrove is an app for controlling robot vacuums that run [Valetudo](https://valetudo.cloud).
This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

## In short

Valrove does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and
no tracking, and it sends nothing to its developer or to anyone else. Everything it knows
stays on your phone and on your own robot.

## What Valrove stores on your phone

- **Your robots:** the name, address and model of each robot you add. If your robot's web
  interface is protected with a username and password, these are stored in Android's
  secure storage, encrypted with a key that never leaves your phone.
- **What you set up in the app:** settings, presets and room icons, and how often you
  have opened the app (for the donation reminder).
- **What the app records about your robots:** the history of cleanups (rooms, area, time,
  battery, the robot's path and obstacles), Wi-Fi signal maps of your home, photos of
  obstacles the robot recognised, and consumable readings for wear forecasts.

This data stays on your phone. Removing a robot in the app deletes its connection details
and password; uninstalling Valrove deletes everything. If Android's backup is turned on,
Android may include the app's data in your backup to your Google account, as it does for
other apps; saved passwords can't be read from such a backup.

## Network connections

Valrove connects only to the robots you add, at the address you give it or the one found
on your local network. It talks to them directly through Valetudo's local API. No data is
sent to the developer of Valrove or to any third party.

The demo robot runs entirely inside the app and makes no connections.

## Microphone

Valrove uses the microphone only while you use voice commands, after you tap the
microphone button. The speech is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition
service (for example Google's), which may process the audio on its own servers under its
own privacy policy. Valrove receives only the recognised text, uses it to carry out the
command, and does not store it.

## Notifications

Valrove shows notifications about running cleanups and your robot (for example when a
cleanup is finished or the dust bin is full). They are created on your phone from the
robot's data.

## Assistants

On Android 16 and newer, assistants on your phone (such as Gemini) can use Valrove's
functions when you ask them to, for example to start a cleanup. The assistant then
receives the result, such as your robot's status, room names or presets. How the assistant
handles that data is described in its own privacy policy.

## Links

Some buttons open websites in your browser, such as the Valetudo documentation or
Valrove's donation page on Ko-fi. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

## Children

Valrove is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
